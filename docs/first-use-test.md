# First-use test: 5 people, 2 minutes, no instructions

**Question:** can someone who has never seen wake make a drawing they like, without being told how?

## Who
Five people who haven't seen wake or uji. Aim for a mix: at least 2 non-designers, at least 1 on a phone.

## Setup
- Open the wake link, already showing the default drawing. Don't open the case study.
- Desktop: screen recording on. Phone: have a second person watch over their shoulder.
- Say only this: **"This is a drawing instrument. You have two minutes. Make something you'd keep."** Then stay quiet. Don't explain anything, even if they ask; answer "what would you try?"

## What to record (per person)
| measure | how |
|---|---|
| time to first change | seconds until the drawing first changes |
| first thing touched | slider / block / handle on the canvas / surprise / seed shape |
| found the stack is reorderable? | yes/no: did they move a block (↑↓ or drag)? |
| found canvas handles? | yes/no |
| kept something? | at 2:00, ask "would you keep this one?" If yes, press share and save the link |
| one-line quote | ask "what is this thing?" and write the answer down verbatim |

## Pass criteria
- 4 of 5 make a change within 15 s.
- 4 of 5 say yes to "would you keep this one?"
- At least 3 of 5 answers to "what is this thing?" mention time, trace, or movement. That's the idea landing without being explained.

## After
Put the 5 kept links and quotes in `docs/first-use-results.md`. Each item that fails the criteria becomes one design change, and then the test is re-run with 5 new people.
